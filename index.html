How it works

From your logo to your wardrobe in four steps.

  1. STEP 1

    Fill in the order form

    Download the form and tell us your garments, sizes, colours, print placement and the date you need them by.

  2. STEP 2

    Send your artwork

    Logos and designs need to be in PNG or PDF format. Need a hand with the design? Just let us know on the form.

  3. STEP 3

    Approve your mock-up

    We'll send you a mock design. Once you approve it, a 50% deposit gets your printing underway.

  4. STEP 4

    Collect or receive

    Choose pick up, drop off or shipping, and your finished order is on its way to you.
